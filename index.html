Why use Video → Scratch?

Frames as Costumes

Every single frame is converted into a Scratch costume. Perfect for stop-motion, pixel art, or just bringing video into your projects.

Audio Extraction

The original soundtrack is automatically extracted and attached to the sprite as a sound. Just press play and watch.

Auto-generated Blocks

No need to code. The converter automatically builds the logic to sync costumes to the timer, so your video plays at the right speed.

Fast & Lightweight

Powered by FFmpeg. Choose your frame rate and quality to get the perfect balance between smooth playback and file size.

How it works

01

Download & Unzip

Grab the VIDEO2SCRATCH archive and extract it to any folder on your PC.

02

Run start.bat

Double-click start.bat. It will automatically fetch FFmpeg if needed and open the app.

03

Load & Convert

Drop your video, tweak the settings, and hit convert. You'll get a ready .sb3 file.
